GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the deadline to sign up?

The Sign up deadline is October 16.

How long can my act be?

Acts should be arouund 2 to 3 minutes long.

Can I perform with friends?

Absolutely. Duets and group performances are welcome.

What kinds of performances are allowed?

Music, dance, comedy, magic, spoken word, skits, and other school-appropriate performances are welcome.

I'm a singer, can I use recorded music?

Singers may be accompanied by an instrumental track only, no singing on track. Singers may NOT sing along to a pre-recorded artist track or any track that already has someone singing your part. The track must be an instrumental or karaoke version.

Can I participate in more than one act?

Performers are limited to one act.

What happens after I sign up?

Refer to the calendar for important dates

If you've sign up before Oct 13, you can still come to the talent workshop!

Next up is the Talent Committee Interview where the committee will help make sure your act is appropriate for the school.
Examples of things the committee will review:

  • Lyrics
  • Dance Moves or Gestures
  • Jokes
  • Scripts
After your talent interview:
If the talent committee asks you to change anything in your act, you must make the necessary changes before the Nov 9 Talent Review.
After the talent review, you can focus on having your act ready for the Nov. 11 rehearsal and the big day on the 13th.

Can I volunteer to help?

Yes, please! We would love your help setting up or breaking down before and after the show. Email gems.talentshow.support@gmail.com
