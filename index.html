GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can my act be?

Acts should be no longer than five minutes. Final timing will be confirmed after registration.

Can I perform with friends?

Absolutely. Duets and group performances are welcome.

What kinds of performances are allowed?

Music, dance, comedy, magic, spoken word, skits, and other school-appropriate performances are welcome.

Can I use recorded music?

Yes. Information about submitting music or audio files will be provided to registered performers.

What happens after I sign up?

The organizers will contact performers with rehearsal, scheduling, and show-day information.
