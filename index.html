Frequently Asked Questions

How long can an act be?

Plan for a maximum of 5 minutes per act. Final timing will be confirmed after registration.

What kinds of performances are allowed?

Music, dance, comedy, magic, spoken word, skits, and other school-appropriate acts are welcome.

Can I perform with friends?

Yes. Duets and groups are welcome. One person should be designated as the primary contact.

Can I use recorded music?

Yes. Details about file format and how to submit music will be provided to registered performers.

What happens after I sign up?

The organizers will review submissions and contact performers with rehearsal, timing, and show-day information.
